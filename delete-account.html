Delete your Daily Focusr account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Daily Focusr and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.

Your account, tasks, categories and check-ins are removed from the server straight away.

Without the app

Email anthonymirely@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". The account and its data will be deleted within 7 days and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, tasks, categories and sign-in tokens.
  • Kept for up to 90 days: weekly database backups that were made before the deletion. They are stored privately, are used only to recover from data loss, and expire on their own.
