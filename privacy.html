Daily Focusr privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Daily Focusr is a task app made by Anthony Mirely. This page says what the app stores about you, why, and how to remove it.

What the app stores

  • Your account. Your email address and, if you sign in with a password, a one-way hash of it. The password itself is never stored. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the app receives your email address and an account identifier from them; it does not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • What you put in. Your tasks (titles, descriptions, due dates, estimates, reminders, the time you say a task took, and focus-timer minutes), your categories, and your answer to the daily "How did today feel?" check-in, if you give one.
  • Sign-in tokens. Short-lived tokens that keep you signed in, stored on your device in its secure storage, and a hashed copy of the refresh token on the server.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking code.

Where it is stored

On servers run by Google Cloud (the app's backend, in the United States) and MongoDB Atlas (the database). A weekly backup of the database is kept privately for 90 days.

Who sees it

Nobody else. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is used only to show your own tasks back to you.

Notifications

Reminders and focus-timer alerts are scheduled on your own device. They are not sent through a server and their content does not leave your phone.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your account, tasks, categories and check-ins from the server straight away. Backups that contain them expire within 90 days.

If you cannot open the app, email anthonymirely@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for deletion. See also the delete-account page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
